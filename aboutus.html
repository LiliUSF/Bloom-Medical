A little about me

Technology is most meaningful when it helps people.

I am Lisandra Kennell, a technology professional with experience in software development, database engineering, and technology. I value thoughtful problem-solving and building practical digital experiences that make information easier to understand and use.

I am pursuing graduate studies in Artificial Intelligence and Business Analytics at the University of South Florida (USF). My studies bring together technology and data, complementing my professional background and interest in useful, human-centered digital tools.

✳

Thoughtful technology, grounded in people. This project reflects my interest in combining clear communication, reliable information, and approachable software in a calm digital experience.

Behind the build

Made simply,
with intention.

This assignment is a static, browser-based prototype—not a live clinic platform.

01

Designed with HTML & CSS

Semantic HTML organizes four connected pages. A shared CSS file provides the responsive layouts, design system, accessible focus states, and mobile navigation styling.

HTML5CSS3
02

Interactions in JavaScript

Vanilla JavaScript powers the appointment form, client-side validation, search and filters, CRUD actions, and a localStorage-backed fictional dataset.

Vanilla JavaScriptlocalStorage
03

Charts with Chart.js

The analytics page loads Chart.js from its public CDN to render responsive department, monthly trend, and appointment status charts using the same sample records.

Chart.js CDNFictional data
